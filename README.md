# Task 7 Ethics and Governance of Synthetic Media

This submission reasons from the two stock-voice audio pipelines in [Task 6](https://github.com/architmalleshaa/Task_06_Deep_Fake/blob/main/README.md) and proposes an operational policy for a hypothetical university sports analytics teaching lab. The setting was selected because the prototype combines quantitative research, discussion of real athletes and a format that could imply a real interview.

## Contents

- [PHASE_A_ANALYSIS.md](PHASE_A_ANALYSIS.md) — return to the artifact, four invented scenarios along truth/consent/context/scale, mitigation analysis and accountability.
- [POLICY.md](POLICY.md) — actual proposed policy covering permitted uses, prohibited uses, consent, disclosure, provenance, review, incident response and refusal.
- [LIMITATIONS.md](LIMITATIONS.md) — stress tests, implementation limits and residual risks.
- The [Task 6 process log](https://github.com/architmalleshaa/Task_06_Deep_Fake/blob/main/PROCESS_LOG.md) and [provenance results](https://github.com/architmalleshaa/Task_06_Deep_Fake/blob/main/provenance_results.json) supply the observed technical evidence. No media are re-uploaded in this folder.

The key observation was ordinary disclosure metadata disappearing after controlled re-encoding. The broader ethical concern is that truthful content in a synthetic interview can imply an endorsement or conversation that never occurred. The resulting policy requires approval of every released excerpt and refuses impersonation, fabricated endorsements and unsupported causal claims even when labeling is present.

The policy is a proposal, not an adopted Syracuse University document. Its staffing, review timelines and retention periods are explicitly chosen governance rules. They are not claims about existing law or institutional practice. No external incident case studies were used. The supplied materials do not establish current legal requirements, named platform commitments or adopted professional codes; those areas are discussed as categories and limitations rather than filled with unsupported assertions.

An unresolved Task 6 limitation carries into this work: perceptual listening review was not performed. The policy's required perceptual review would therefore block public release until a qualified listener completes that check. This is a direct application of the policy to the prototype, rather than a claim that the prototype automatically meets its own standards.
